# Sun Shield Support

Sun Shield 1.0 helps choose the side of a car with less predicted direct sun for a driving route.

## Common fixes

- **Current Location does not work:** Location is optional. Type a starting place instead, or enable location access for Sun Shield in iOS Settings and retry.
- **A place cannot be found:** Try a more specific suburb, address, or place name.
- **A route cannot be calculated:** Check your internet connection and try again. The routing service may occasionally be unavailable.
- **The result looks different in real life:** Sun Shield models route direction, travel time, and sun position. It does not currently model buildings, trees, clouds, tunnels, window tint, or exact lane position.

## Privacy

Sun Shield does not create user accounts or store location history. See [PRIVACY.md](./PRIVACY.md).

## Report a problem

Open a GitHub issue and include your device model, iOS version, what you entered, and what happened.
